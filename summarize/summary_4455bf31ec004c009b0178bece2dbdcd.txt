 Database Management Systems find applications across various domains and industries due to their ability to efficiently store, retrieve, and manage large volumes of data . DBMS are used to manage customer accounts, transactions, loans, and financial records securely .  Retailers, warehouses, and manufacturers rely on DBMS to track inventory levels, orders, supply chain, and logistics . Government agencies use DBMS for managing citizen data, tax records, land registries, and public services .  In DBMS, information refers to processed and organized data that provides insights and context for decision-making . Metadata in DBMS refers to data that describes the structure, organization, and characteristics of the data .  DBMS enables users to store, retrieve, update, and manage data efficiently while ensuring data integrity, security, and concurrency control . It serves as an interface between users and the underlying database, providing tools and utilities for managing data .
